Burst pipe

When a burst pipe is an emergency

A split pipe can release hundreds of gallons in an hour. The main valve and the breaker are the two things that limit the damage.

  1. 1
    Shut the main water valve. It is near the meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. 2
    Cut power to wet areas. Use the breaker, not a switch in a wet room.
  3. 3
    Open a low faucet. This drains remaining water from the pipes.
  4. 4
    Call and ask about help. Tell us where the water is coming from and whether it has stopped.
Causes

Why pipes burst in Florida homes

Freezing is rare in Central Florida, but burst pipes still happen. Corrosion, high pressure, a failed fitting, tree root pressure on buried lines and shifting soil under a slab are the common causes. Occasional hard freezes can also catch unprotected outdoor lines.

Check your water pressure if you have had more than one failure. High pressure stresses every pipe and fixture in the house, and a pressure regulator is an inexpensive fix.

Repairs

Repair methods for copper, PEX and PVC

A split in copper is cut out and a new section is soldered or joined with push fittings. PEX is repaired with crimp or expansion fittings. PVC and CPVC use solvent-welded couplings.

If the pipe is old and brittle, patching one split may only move the failure to the next weak point. Where several lines are corroded, a repipe is the long-term option.

Water damage

After the water stops

Once the pipe is fixed, standing water and wet materials need drying. Take photos of the damage and keep receipts for any claim. A restoration company can dry walls and floors, and mold can start in a few days if materials stay wet.

If the burst was behind a wall or under a slab, leak detection and slab leak repair explain how we find hidden failures before they flood again.

Local

Burst pipes in Sanford homes

Many homes around Sanford sit on slabs, so a burst line may be under the floor, in the attic or inside a wall. Attic lines can leak onto a ceiling and bring down drywall before you notice.

Do not wait for water to stop on its own. A pipe that has burst once is usually one that will fail again, and agreeing on a price before work starts keeps the job simple.

Rules and references

Codes that apply to a burst pipe repair

According to published code guidelines, plumbing work in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code. A repair that replaces a section of supply line usually has to match the pipe material and pressure rating of the rest of the system.

Whether a permit is needed depends on the size of the repair and on the city or county, so ask the local building department about your address.
