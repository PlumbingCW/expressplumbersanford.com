While you arrange help

If you have a burst pipe or an active leak in Lake Mary

A burst pipe can release a lot of water in a short time, and a house built on a slab spreads it across the floor. These four steps limit the damage while you arrange help.

  1. 1
    Close the main water valve. It is near the meter or where the line enters the house.
  2. 2
    Shut off power to wet rooms. Use the breaker, never a switch with wet hands.
  3. 3
    Clear the area. Move rugs, electronics and valuables above the waterline.
  4. 4
    Call. Give your Lake Mary address and what you have already shut off.
