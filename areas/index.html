Sanford

Sanford, our home base

Sanford is the county seat of Seminole County and sits on the south shore of Lake Monroe. We serve ZIP codes 32771 and 32773, plus the post office box ZIP 32772, from Historic Downtown and Goldsboro to the Seminole Towne Center area and the Orlando Sanford International Airport side of town.

Older homes near First Street and the RiverWalk often have mixed plumbing from several decades. Newer subdivisions along SR 46 and US 17-92 more often need water heater and fixture work. See the Sanford homepage for how calls work.

Nearby towns

Lake Mary and Longwood

Our Lake Mary emergency plumber page covers ZIP codes 32746 and 32795, and the Longwood addresses in 32750 and 32779 are handled through the same phone line. Both towns sit along Interstate 4 north of Sanford.

Lake Mary and Longwood run their own utilities and have their own water quality reports, so ask your utility if you need hardness or chlorine numbers.

Calls

How we cover Seminole County

Every call goes to one phone line, and the job is matched to a plumber by street address. Sanford addresses are the shortest drive, and Lake Mary and Longwood are a short run up Interstate 4 or along US 17-92.

Traffic on I-4 and SR 46 can change a drive time, so we do not promise a fixed arrival. We give you the best estimate we have and we call if it changes. The Sanford and Lake Mary areas have different housing ages, so describe your house when you call.

If you are between towns, such as the Heathrow side of Lake Mary or the eastern edge of Longwood, give the exact street address and cross street. That detail speeds up booking and avoids a wrong turn.
