What the record shows

A community with its own town charter

The Goldsboro Museum says the town was incorporated on December 1, 1891, by William Clark, a store owner, and 19 other registered Black voters. The museum describes it as the second Black incorporated city in Florida. Its first mayor was Walter Williams.

The same history places Goldsboro near the railroad yards where citrus and celery were loaded for northern markets. In 1911 the state legislature revoked the town's incorporation and Sanford absorbed it. The museum, the welcome center and the Crooms Academy Museum now stand along Historic Goldsboro Boulevard.

Why this matters for a plumbing call: Goldsboro has been inside the City of Sanford for over a century, so questions about city water, sewer service and permits go to Sanford, not to a separate town.

General information

Common plumbing questions, whatever the age of the house

  • Slow drain in one fixture: Often a local clog. Drain cleaning is the usual first step.
  • Water bill climbing with no change in use: A hidden leak is possible. Leak detection finds it before anything is opened.
  • Water heater leaking or past its expected life: Compare repair with replacement after a plumber inspects it.
  • Sewer smell or backups in several drains: A sewer camera inspection shows what the main line looks like inside.
Planning ahead

Before you buy or remodel a home here

If you are buying a house in West Sanford, ask for a sewer camera inspection during the inspection period. If you are remodeling, plumbing installation covers moved fixtures and added bathrooms, and a plumber can tell you what the permit office will want to see.

Because Goldsboro sits within the city, the City of Sanford is the place to ask about permits and utility service for your address.

Practical note

Permits and code, in one paragraph

Before a remodel or a water heater swap, ask the City of Sanford whether the job needs a permit. The Florida Building Code, Plumbing sets the rules for the work itself, and your plumber can tell you who files the paperwork.
