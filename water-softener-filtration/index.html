Start with a test

Know what is in your water before you buy

Hardness, chlorine taste, iron staining and sediment are different problems with different equipment. A softener removes the minerals that cause scale, a carbon filter removes chlorine taste and odor, and a sediment filter catches particles.

The City of Sanford's water quality report lists chlorine, fluoride and lead but does not state hardness, so test your tap or ask the utility for the numbers. A home test kit or a lab test gives a baseline before any system is sized.

Local water

What Sanford's utility reports

According to the City of Sanford's annual water quality report, the city draws drinking water from seventeen groundwater wells in the Floridan Aquifer and disinfects it with chlorine. Chlorine is the reason many households choose a carbon filter for taste.

Lake Mary and Longwood have their own utilities and their own reports. Ask your utility for the one that covers your address, because water can differ between systems.

Softeners

How a water softener works

A softener holds resin beads that trade calcium and magnesium for sodium or potassium. When the resin is full, the system regenerates by flushing with brine and sending the minerals to the drain.

A softener needs a drain, a power outlet and salt. It protects heaters, faucets and fixtures from scale, and it is often installed next to the water heater. Our water heater installation page covers sizing and clearances nearby.

Service

Leaks, resin and valve repair

Softeners and filters fail at fittings, bypass valves, resin tanks and drains. A leak near a filter housing or a softener head is a plumbing repair, and we can service it or replace the part.

If a system leaks while you are away, shut the bypass or the main water valve first. A small leak at a fitting can run for weeks before anyone notices.

Installation

Where a softener or filter goes in the plumbing

A whole-house system is installed where the main supply enters the home, usually in the garage or a utility room, before the water heater. That placement treats every tap and protects the heater. We add a bypass valve so the system can be isolated for service without shutting off the house.

A softener also needs a drain for its regeneration cycle and a nearby outlet. Where the garage floor has no drain, we plan a standpipe or a drain line to the nearest legal discharge point. Under-sink filters are simpler and often take less than a visit to set up.

Honest limits

What softeners and filters do not do

A softener does not remove chlorine and is not a drinking water purifier. A carbon filter does not remove minerals. No single device covers every concern, and we say so plainly rather than sell more equipment than the water needs.

If you have a specific health concern about your water, talk to your physician and your utility. We handle the plumbing side, and we do not make health claims about drinking water.
