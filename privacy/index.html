Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call us or visit this website.

Information we collect

This website has no forms and does not ask you to create an account. We receive information when you call us, such as your phone number, your address and a description of the plumbing problem, so we can talk about your plumbing problem.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use it

We use call information to book and complete service, to answer questions about the job and to improve how we handle calls. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

Pages on this site load fonts from Google Fonts and may load maps from Google Maps. Those services can receive your IP address and browser details when the page loads, and their own privacy policies apply.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by calling (407) 964-8940 or emailing contact@expressplumbersanford.com.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the date at the bottom of the page shows the latest revision.
