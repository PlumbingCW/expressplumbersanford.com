Plumbing Services in Sanford, FL

Pick the problem you have and we show how the job works, what affects the price and what to do while you wait. Call about any service below, and ask about help with an emergency.

Drains and sewers

  • Drain cleaning clears clogged sinks, tubs and main lines with snaking and cabling.
  • Hydro jetting scours grease, scale and roots from drain and sewer pipes.
  • Sewer line cleaning opens a backed-up main building drain.
  • Sewer camera inspection shows what is inside the line before you dig or buy.
  • Sewer line repair covers spot repair, trenchless lining and replacement.

Water heaters

  • Water heater repair fixes leaks, elements, thermostats and pilot problems.
  • Water heater replacement swaps a failed tank, often quickly when parts are on hand.
  • Water heater installation plans a new unit, a new location or a fuel change.
  • Tankless water heater service covers installation, repair and descale flushing.

Leaks and pipes

  • Leak detection finds hidden leaks in walls, slabs, yards and meters.
  • Slab leak repair locates and fixes leaks under concrete floors.
  • Burst pipe repair stops flooding and replaces the damaged section.
  • Water service line repair fixes the pipe between the meter and the house.
  • Repiping replaces worn supply lines throughout the house.

Fixtures, installs and businesses

  • Toilet repair and installation handles overflows, leaks, flanges and new toilets.
  • Faucet repair covers drips, supply lines, stuck valves and tub fixtures.
  • Garbage disposal repair and installation fixes jams and leaks and sets new units.
  • Plumbing installation adds bathrooms, moves fixtures and roughs in remodels.
  • Water softener and filtration systems are tested, sized and installed.
  • Commercial plumbing keeps restaurants, shops and offices running after hours.
