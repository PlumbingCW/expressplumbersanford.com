Tankless Water Heater Service in Sanford, FL

A tankless water heater heats water as it flows instead of storing it, which saves floor space and ends the cold-shower-after-the-guests problem. We install, repair and flush tankless units in Sanford. Call and ask about help.

How it works

What a tankless water heater does differently

When you open a hot tap, flow sensors tell the burner or elements to fire, and water is heated as it passes through a heat exchanger. Nothing sits in a tank, so there is no standing water to rust and no standby heat loss.

The catch is capacity. A unit can only raise water temperature by so much at a given flow rate, so showers, a dishwasher and a washer running together can exceed what it can deliver. Sizing it to the household is the first job.

Local fit

Warm Florida inlet water helps, and flushing still matters

Water entering a Florida home is warmer than in cold northern states, which usually means the heater has less temperature rise to make. That can mean a smaller unit does the job, but we still size by flow and gas supply, not by guesswork.

Minerals in the water coat the heat exchanger over time. Sanford's annual water quality report lists chlorine, fluoride and lead results and does not state a hardness figure, so test your tap or ask the utility, and plan on flushing the unit on a regular schedule.

Flush and descale

Flushing and descaling a tankless unit

A flush circulates a mild descaling solution through the heat exchanger so scale does not choke flow or trigger error codes. It also lets us clean the inlet filter and check the venting.

If your unit shows a flow, temperature or ignition error, scale is a frequent cause. A flush is cheaper than a replacement heat exchanger, and we can pair it with a check of the gas supply during the same visit.

Installation

What a tankless installation involves

Many tankless conversions need a bigger gas line, a dedicated vent and new water connections with isolation valves for flushing. Electric units need heavy circuits that some panels cannot supply.

If you are swapping a failed tank, compare costs with a standard water heater replacement before committing, since the added gas, vent and electrical work can change the total. Planned work in a new location is part of water heater installation.

Repairs

Tankless problems we see most

The most common complaints are a unit that shows an error code, water that turns cold in the middle of a shower, a unit that will not ignite and reduced flow at the taps. Each points to a different cause, such as a scaled heat exchanger, a blocked inlet filter, a gas supply restriction or a failed sensor.

Cold water sandwiches, where hot water is followed by a cold slug, usually come from a short run time between uses or from a flow too low to trigger the burner. We test flow and temperature at the unit and at the fixture before we recommend a repair or a different setup.
