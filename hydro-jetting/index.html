How it works

What hydro jetting actually does

A jetting machine pumps water at high pressure through a hose with a nozzle that fires jets forward and backward. The backward jets pull the hose along the pipe while the water strips grease, scale and soft debris from the walls instead of punching a single hole through it.

That is the difference from a cable. A cable clears a path, and jetting cleans the pipe. Where the line is sound, the result is a pipe that flows like it was lately installed.

Urgent backup

Sewer or drain backup emergency: your first five minutes

A line that backs up into a tub or floor drain needs action before it reaches carpet or wood floors.

  1. 1
    Stop water use. No flushing, laundry or dishwasher until the line is clear.
  2. 2
    Locate the cleanout. It is a capped pipe near the house or the street side of the yard.
  3. 3
    Keep kids and pets back. Backed-up wastewater carries bacteria and should be handled with gloves.
  4. 4
    Call and say what drains are affected. The plumber decides whether jetting or cabling is the right first pass.
When to choose it

When jetting beats cabling

Jetting earns its price on grease-heavy lines such as restaurant kitchens and older home kitchen branches, on lines coated with mineral scale, and on mains that clog again within months of cabling. It also prepares a line for a clean camera inspection, since a pipe full of debris hides defects.

Jetting is not the right tool for every line. A cracked, collapsed or heavily root-invaded older pipe can be damaged by high pressure, so we look first with a sewer camera when the line's condition is unknown.

Roots

Roots, scale and grease: what jetting can and cannot do

Rotating nozzles and root-cutting heads handle fibrous roots, and a jetting pass afterward washes the cuttings away. Mineral scale breaks up under pressure, and grease liquefies as the hot water and pressure act on it.

What jetting cannot do is repair a broken pipe. If roots return fast, the joint they entered is open, and the lasting fix is sewer line repair rather than another cleaning.

What to do while you arrange help

Preparing for a hydro jetting visit

Keep people and pets away from the cleanout area, and clear the path to the access point. Do not run water or flush while the line is being jetted.

Tell the plumber about any known cracks, a past repair or a line you suspect is cast iron or clay. High water pressure is a poor choice for a damaged pipe, and the guidelines from equipment makers call for a camera look first when the condition is unknown.

Rules and references

Where jetting fits within code

Jetting is a cleaning method, not a repair, so it does not change what the Florida Building Code, Plumbing requires of the line. If the camera shows a failed section, that section needs repair, and your building department sets any permit rules.
