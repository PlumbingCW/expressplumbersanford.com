Active leak

Leaking water heater emergency: shut it down safely

Water pooling around a tank can come from a loose fitting, a failed relief valve or a tank that has rusted through. The first two are repairs, and the third usually means replacement, but all three start with the same shutdown.

  1. 1
    Turn off the fuel. Set a gas unit to pilot or off, or flip the dedicated breaker for an electric one.
  2. 2
    Close the cold supply. The valve on the pipe feeding the top of the tank stops new water entering.
  3. 3
    Contain the water. Move stored items, and do not step into water that reaches an outlet or power strip.
  4. 4
    Call. Describe where the water is coming from, since fittings, relief valves and tank bodies need different parts.
Symptoms

No hot water, lukewarm water and strange noises

On an electric heater, no hot water at all often points to a tripped breaker, a failed upper element or a bad thermostat, while lukewarm water that runs out fast usually means the lower element has failed. On a gas heater, a pilot that will not stay lit often traces to a worn thermocouple or a dirty burner.

Popping and rumbling come from sediment on the tank bottom. A sulfur smell can point to a reaction at the anode rod. Each has a different fix, which is why we ask a few questions before the truck rolls.

Repairs we make

Common water heater repairs

Most repair calls are small parts: heating elements, thermostats, thermocouples, gas control valves, anode rods, drain valves and temperature and pressure relief valves. A relief valve that drips is doing its job or has failed, and either way it should not be capped or plugged.

If the tank itself is leaking, repair is not possible, because the steel liner has failed. In that case we explain the options under water heater replacement and ask for a price before any work starts.

Gas, electric and hybrid

Gas, electric and hybrid units need different checks

Gas heaters need a clean burn, a working draft and a secure vent, and any smell of gas means leaving the house and calling the gas utility before calling us. Electric heaters are about elements, thermostats and power, and a licensed plumber should confirm the circuit before opening any panel.

Heat pump or hybrid units add a compressor, filters and a condensate drain, so a unit that blows cool air or drips from the base has more to inspect. For homes considering a switch to on-demand hot water, tankless water heater service covers install, repair and flushing.

Local

Water heaters in Sanford garages, closets and attics

Many Central Florida homes sit on concrete slabs, so tanks often live in the garage, a utility closet or the attic. Attic units can leak through a ceiling before anyone notices, and garage units face dust, humidity and sometimes flooding during heavy summer storms.

Sanford's drinking water comes from groundwater wells that draw from the Floridan Aquifer, according to the City of Sanford's annual water quality report. The report does not give a hardness figure, so test your tap or ask the utility, because mineral buildup shortens heater life and makes sediment noises more likely.
